Module 2 & 3 // Algorithmic Architecture

Defensible Edge Intelligence Innovations

INNOVATION 1

HPGM

Hierarchical Planogram Geometric Mapping

Maps physical multi-tier shelving into normalized 2D coordinate polygons. Isolates active inventory regions from ceiling/floor pixels, reducing spatial inference compute surface by up to 60%.

INNOVATION 2

CSIM

Centroid-Shelf Interaction Matrix

Continuous spatial-temporal filter monitoring human-shelf intersection vectors. Suppresses false out-of-stock alarms when customer overlaps shelf by >10%, governed by a 2.0s debounce state machine.

INNOVATION 3

TMPS

Temporal Multi-Task Pipeline Splitting

Asynchronously multiplexes person tracking (10–15 Hz), queue dwell estimation (2 Hz), and shelf depletion checks (0.1 Hz). Keeps Raspberry Pi 5 SoC duty cycle below 40% within a sub-12W thermal envelope.

Module 1 & 7 // Privacy by Design

DPDP Act 2023 Compliance & Data Minimization

Volatile DMA-BUF Only

Frame transformations reside exclusively in RAM direct memory access buffers. Pixels are overwritten immediately after tensor extraction (<33ms).

No Biometric Ingestion

Zero facial recognition or biometric embeddings. The vision pipeline outputs solely anonymized bounding boxes, trajectory vectors, and zone counters.

Zero WAN Leakage

No video clips or images ever touch persistent storage or external wide-area networks. Meets full DPDP 2023 regulations for commercial public spaces.
